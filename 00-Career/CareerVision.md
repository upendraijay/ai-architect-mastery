# Career Vision

## Why Forward Deployed Architect?

I feel this role matches what I already enjoy doing. I like talking to customers, understanding their problems, and then building solutions that they can actually use.

For example, in my AEM DAM project, I first understood the customer's challenges and then designed and built an Agentic AI solution to solve them. I enjoy working on both the business side and the technical side rather than focusing on just one.

I also believe the biggest challenge in AI today is not building models—it's deploying them successfully in real enterprise environments and making them deliver real business value.

Finally, with 12 years of experience in enterprise projects, I can communicate effectively with business stakeholders, understand enterprise constraints, and build solutions that work in production. That's why I believe the Forward Deployed Architect role is a great fit for me.

---

## 5-Year Target (by 2031)

My goal over the next five years is to become a Principal-level Forward Deployed Architect who owns a business domain, not just individual customer engagements. I also want to stay hands-on with technology because I enjoy building, so I'd like to spend at least 50% of my time designing and developing solutions.

---

## Target Companies

I'm looking for product companies where AI is solving real customer problems and where engineers work directly with customers to build production solutions.

**Tier 1 — Frontier AI Labs (Long-term Goal):** OpenAI, Anthropic

These companies are defining the future of AI. My goal is to eventually work on frontier AI systems and large-scale agentic applications.

**Tier 2 — Enterprise AI Product Companies (Primary Target):** Palantir, Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft, Google Cloud, AWS, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Glean, Scale AI, NVIDIA, Adobe

These are my preferred companies because they combine AI, enterprise software, and customer-facing architecture. The work is technically challenging and has a direct business impact.

**Tier 3 — Global Product Companies with Strong India Presence:** Atlassian, Intuit, Postman, Rubrik, Harness, Freshworks, BrowserStack, Zoho, Cisco, Nutanix

These companies build world-class products from India and offer opportunities to work on large-scale engineering problems.

**Tier 4 — AI-First Startups:** Sarvam AI, Krutrim, Yellow.ai, DevRev, Observe.AI, Kore.ai

## Problems I Enjoy Solving

- Turning ambiguity into architecture – taking unclear business requirements and designing scalable, production-ready AI solutions.
- Debugging complex systems – investigating non-obvious failures, finding root causes, and making AI systems more reliable.
